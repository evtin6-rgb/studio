Case notes — how this concept was designed

A portfolio where projects are chapters, not thumbnails.

Brief

A fictional interior studio whose work is about material and light. The usual grid of tiny photos and a list of services flattens exactly what makes them good.

Decisions

  • Each project is a chapter with facts (area, time, year), a palette and a plan you can explore.
  • A material library replaces “our services” — it shows taste instead of claiming it.
  • Calm serif, stone and olive tones, lots of air. No carousel anywhere.

What you'd get

  • Chapter-based project template you can reuse for every new case
  • Interactive plan and material blocks, ready for real photos
  • Clear process section that pre-answers client questions
