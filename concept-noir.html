Case notes — how this concept was designed

A store that reads like a magazine cover, then sells like a shop.

Brief

A fictional independent label with twelve pieces per season. It needs a site that feels like the brand, not like a template store with a logo on top.

Decisions

  • Black, bone and one signal red — nothing else competes with the product.
  • Condensed display type for attitude, a quiet sans for anything you have to read.
  • Scarcity is shown honestly (“Last 4”) instead of fake countdown timers.

What you'd get

  • Art direction and a reusable product grid
  • Filters, size selection and bag logic ready for Shopify or Snipcart
  • Responsive build, EN/RU copy structure
