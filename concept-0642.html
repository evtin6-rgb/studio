Case notes — how this concept was designed

A local brand told through time, not through stock photos of latte art.

Brief

A tiny fictional roastery that opens when the first batch is ready. It needs to feel warm and specific, and answer “is it open, what's on, what's good today” in three seconds.

Decisions

  • The name is a time, so the whole site is built around a clock: live open status and a timeline that highlights the current hour.
  • Menu before story. Prices are visible, the story comes after.
  • Soft serif, paper tones and one terracotta accent instead of the usual brown-and-gold coffee look.

What you'd get

  • Identity-led one-pager with an editable menu
  • Live opening hours and a small useful tool (brew calculator)
  • Map, beans shop or delivery link when needed
