/ 08 — FAQ

Before
you ask.

How long does it take?

A landing page usually takes 2–3 weeks, a full website 4–6 weeks. You get the exact timeline with the quote; it mostly depends on how quickly copy and images are ready.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions at the design stage. That's usually enough, because the structure is agreed first. Extra rounds are possible and quoted up front.

What about domain and hosting?

The domain and hosting accounts are in your name, so you own everything. I set them up and launch the site on your domain. For most small sites hosting is free or close to it.

What happens after launch?

I can stay on for fixes, new sections and improvements. Support isn't bundled into the price — it's agreed separately, per task or per month, only if you need it.
