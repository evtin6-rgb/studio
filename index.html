Questions people ask

How long does it take?

A landing page usually takes 2–3 weeks, a full website 4–6. I'll give you exact dates together with the price. It mostly depends on how quickly the copy and photos are ready.

How many changes can I ask for?

Two rounds of changes at the design stage. That's usually enough, because we agree on the structure first. If you need more, I'll tell you the price up front.

What about the domain and hosting?

Both go in your name, so everything belongs to you. I set it all up and launch the site on your domain. For a small site, hosting usually costs nothing or close to it.

What happens after launch?

If you need it, I can keep making fixes, adding sections and improving the site. That isn't part of the website price. We agree on it separately, per task or per month.

I don't have copy or photos. Is that a problem?

No. I'll tell you what's needed and in what order. I can write the copy myself, but that's priced separately.
