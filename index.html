You always know what stage we're at, what I need from you and what comes next.

    Brief

    A short call or written brief: goal, audience, what isn't working. You get a fixed price and timeline before anything starts.

  2. 023–5 days

    What goes where and why: page structure, message hierarchy and the one action each page should lead to.

/ 08 — FAQ

Before
you ask.

How long does it take?

A landing page usually takes 2–3 weeks, a full website 4–6. I'll give you exact dates together with the price. Most of it depends on how quickly copy and photos are ready.

How many revisions are included?

Two rounds of revisions at the design stage. That's usually enough, because we agree on the structure first. If you need more, I'll name the price before we start.

What about domain and hosting?

The domain and hosting are registered in your name, so everything belongs to you. I set it all up and launch the site on your domain. For a small site, hosting is usually free or close to it.

What happens after launch?

I can stay on for fixes, new sections and improvements. Support isn't part of the website price. We agree on it separately, per task or per month, if you need it.
